01Deep Learning · IIT Bombay · 2022–23

Exploring Multi-Modal Deep Learning

Enhancing vision-based Deep Metric Learning and Out-of-Distribution Detection through language integration.

THE PROBLEM

Traditional vision models can miss higher-level semantic relationships. The project explored whether language representations could provide useful guidance to visual models.

THE APPROACH

We explored Language Guidance, introduced Hint Learning for Deep Metric Learning, and combined Maximum Concept Matching with Prompt Learning for OOD detection.

RESULTS

Prompt Learning + MCM improved OOD detection by approximately 1–2% over MCM alone in the reported experiments. The Oxford-Pet evaluation reported 99.62% AUROC and 1.70% FPR95 for MCM.

WHAT I LEARNED

The interesting part was not just model accuracy: it was designing experiments, comparing approaches, interpreting failure modes and understanding why results changed with training choices.
